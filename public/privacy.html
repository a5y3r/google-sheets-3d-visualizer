Privacy Policy

Google Sheets 3D Visualizer is a demo project. This page explains what happens to your data when you sign in.

What we receive

When you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with this page.

How it is used

This information is only used to show who is signed in. It is kept in your browser's session storage and is deleted when you sign out or close the tab.

What we do not do

This site has no backend server. Your information is not sent to, stored on, or shared with any server or third party by this site. We do not access your Google Drive, Sheets, or any other Google data.
